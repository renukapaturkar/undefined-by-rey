title: "Learnings from 2025"
publishedAt: '2025-12-31'
summary: ''
---


- **Be opinionated.** We're all opinionated at heart,don't dilute yours just to fit in.

- **Cultivate tastes that are truly yours.** Seek out what you genuinely like, unapologetically.

- **Your Dreams aren't rare;** The depth you pour in from your soul sets them apart.
- **Try new things relentlessly.** Dive into your domain, your industry, and far beyond—what you explore circles back to rescue you in unexpected ways.
- **Capture the good moments. specially the good ones!** Bad times sear into memory; the joyful ones fade. Write them, film them, record them—for the gift to your future self.
- **Love freely, but wisely.** Everything in life deserves your care and affection, none of it is wasted. Just know when it's time to let go.
- **Meet others as humans, nothing more.** *(Inspired by Carl Jung)*
- **Forgive yourself.** You don't want to despise the one person you share a bed with every night: yourself.
- **Buy more books.**
- **Nurture body and mind in tandem.** Your mental health hinges on physical well-being, and vice versa. Tend to both without fail.
- **Accept life's emptiness, but value the links.** Life can feel pointless, with everything fading away in the end, now more than ever. But don't call it meaningless: people have figured out its truths for thousands of years and passed on their wisdom. Draw your purpose from that enduring legacy.
- **Humility saves you and the world.** Achievements can make you feel invincible, but true grace lies in staying grounded.
